Built for coast living

Salt air, fog and winter storms

Life on the Central Oregon Coast is hard on a home's comfort systems. Here's how we can help.

Salt-air corrosion

Ocean spray and salty fog wear on outdoor units and exposed metal. Regular maintenance helps catch corrosion early. Ask about a yearly maintenance agreement.

AI-generated illustration

Damp-coast humidity & mold

Wet winters mean condensation and musty air. Heat recovery ventilators and whole-home air cleaners bring in fresh air and cut down on mold, mildew and allergens.

AI-generated illustration

Storm power outages

Heat pumps for a mild climate

The coast's mild, damp weather suits heat pumps and ductless systems, which can be an efficient upgrade from baseboard or wall heaters.

Rebates you may qualify for

Central Lincoln PUD lists rebates for ductless heat pumps, air-source heat pumps, smart thermostats and heat pump water heaters. You may qualify, so check eligibility with Central Lincoln PUD.

Our story

A coast family business since 1947
